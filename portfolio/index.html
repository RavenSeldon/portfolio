Multi-agent research · Absent stakeholders

The Seventh Seat ↗

Who protects the party that isn’t at the table?

Explore the observatory ↗

Six AI district agents share a synthetic watershed; a downstream community has no agent of its own. A factorial experiment tests whether verified evidence and direct reporting help protect that absent party.

96 evaluation episodes · 24 matched scenarios · One model configuration

Neither primary hypothesis met the preregistered support rule. These results do not establish protection for real communities.

Monitoring
Under Scarcity

Explore the Sīdus programme ↗

How do resource-bounded intelligent systems decide which internal information gets to influence behaviour?

Ground-truth computational studies separating information available inside a system from information that actually changes what it does.

E0-ACompleted

Information → causal use

Exact enumeration, eight adversarial controls, and 500 retained training initializations test the distinction.

E0-A results ↗
E0-BCompleted

What counts as monitoring?

Three operational accounts tested. One achieved bounded functional validation; two remained non-identified.

E0-B results ↗
E1Working paper

Monitoring under a budget

Completed synthetic study of shared-budget monitoring; independent replication pending. Read the findings and limitations.

E1 findings & paper ↗
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24025391 ↗
Key finding / E0-A

An informative-but-unused control carried the same hidden-state information as a successful system, with zero behavioural benefit. Information alone was not enough.
